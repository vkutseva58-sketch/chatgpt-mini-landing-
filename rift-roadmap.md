# Пепельный разлом

Original mobile MOBA prototype. Open /rift.html. No external art/runtime dependencies; procedural Canvas 2.5D graphics.

Stage 1: one lane, animated Ember hero, three skills, minion waves, opposing bot, towers and cores, gold/levels/upgrades, death/respawn, victory, pause, touch stick and desktop keys. Local match only.

Next: refine silhouettes, add guardian and healer with distinct abilities, map with jungle/objectives, stronger enemy AI, balancing and mobile performance testing. Online multiplayer requires authoritative server and separate architecture.

Art direction: ruined stone suspended over teal abyss, warm ember light, readable stylized characters, glowing crystals. Preserve existing site and games.
